About the Project

Why Northeast India?

Northeast India is one of the most flood-prone regions in the world. Every year, the mighty Brahmaputra and its tributaries inundate vast areas of Assam, displacing millions of people, destroying crops, and causing irreversible damage to infrastructure and ecosystems.

Traditional flood monitoring methods are too slow and too coarse to provide actionable early warnings. FloodSense AI changes this by combining real-time satellite data, ground sensor networks, and state-of-the-art machine learning models trained specifically on the unique hydrology of Northeast India.

How It Works

The FloodSense Pipeline

🛰️
01

Data Collection

Satellite imagery (Sentinel-1 SAR, MODIS), IoT river gauge sensors, weather station data, and rainfall forecasts are aggregated in real time.

→
⚙️
02

AI Processing

Our multi-modal deep learning model (CNN + LSTM hybrid) analyzes terrain, water levels, soil saturation, and rainfall intensity simultaneously.

→
🔮
03

Flood Prediction

The model outputs flood probability maps, predicted inundation extent, and risk scores for every district — up to 72 hours in advance.

→
🚨
04

Alert Dispatch

Automated alerts are sent via SMS, app notifications, and government dashboards to trigger timely evacuation and resource deployment.

Features

What Makes FloodSense Unique

🧠

Multi-Modal AI

Combines CNNs for spatial pattern recognition with LSTMs for temporal sequence modeling — purpose-built for hydrological prediction.

🗺️

High-Resolution Flood Maps

30-meter resolution inundation maps updated every 6 hours, allowing village-level risk assessment and targeted evacuations.

⚡

72-Hour Early Warning

Industry-leading 3-day advance warning gives communities ample time to evacuate, move livestock, and secure assets.

🌐

Offline-First Dashboard

Works even with poor internet connectivity — critical for remote areas of Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, and Manipur.

🔗

Government Integration

Direct API integration with NDRF, Assam SDMA, and State Disaster Management portals for coordinated emergency response.

📊

Historical Analytics

30+ years of flood data visualized for trend analysis, climate change impact assessment, and infrastructure planning.
